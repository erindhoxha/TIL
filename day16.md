One article about "Becoming unblockable"

This article is about how engineers can structure their work and environment to always make forward progress, avoiding
being stalled by blockers. Key strategies include:

Work on multiple tasks: Keep at least one non-blocked task available so you can switch when another is blocked. Avoid
juggling two urgent tasks at the same time.

Sequence work strategically: Identify likely blockers early (e.g., migrations, controversial decisions) so the rest of
your project can continue while waiting.

Prioritize a stable developer environment.

Fix issues in your dev setup immediately: Avoid over-customized tooling and be creative when things break.

Debug outside your domain: Investigate issues in other services yourself, using logs, code, or AI tools before asking
for help.

Build relationships: Strong connections with engineers on other teams allow faster collaboration and unblocking.

Leverage senior allies: Senior managers can help unblock high-priority tasks if you clearly communicate what you need.
